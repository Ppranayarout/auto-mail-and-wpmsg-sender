# WhatsApp & Instagram DM Templates for BBSR Businesses

In India (especially in Bhubaneswar), local cafes, restaurants, gyms, and clinic reception desks respond much faster to **WhatsApp** and **Instagram DMs** than cold emails.

---

### 1. Cafe & Restaurant Template (WhatsApp / Instagram DM)
> "Hi Team [Name]! Loved the vibe and food at your [Area, e.g. Patia / KIIT Road] outlet. I'm a web developer from BBSR. Noticed you don't have a direct mobile-friendly website to show your digital menu & take table reservations directly (without 30% aggregator commissions). I've prepared a quick 2-minute demo preview of how your website could look. Can I share the link with you?"
